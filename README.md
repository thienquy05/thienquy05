## About

I'm a Computer Science student focused on **cybersecurity and AI security**, and most of my week is real security operations, not just reading about them.

As an **IT System Administrator at the University of Toledo** (since Jan 2026), I triage security alerts and enforce device compliance across **1,000+ Windows and macOS endpoints** through Active Directory and Microsoft Intune. In four months I responded to **15+ security incidents**, including remediating unpatched Windows 10 machines with open vulnerabilities and isolating 2 malware-infected devices from the network. I also own the endpoint lifecycle (imaging, deployment, patching, hardware refresh) and provide Tier 2 support for 250+ classrooms and offices.

On the AI side, I red-teamed an LLM phishing detector against 41 real attack payloads, and as an **Undergraduate Research Assistant** I build Python/OpenCV video-tracking and behavior-analysis pipelines for zebrafish Novel Tank Test footage.

- Enforcing device compliance and triaging security alerts on a live enterprise fleet
- Building detection and defense tooling in Python
- Studying for **CompTIA Security+**, practicing on TryHackMe, HackTheBox and Splunk
- Graduating **December 2027**
